### 2. Arm-regeneration RT-qPCR lead (CIAC 2025)

`SOURCE-REPORTED_RESULT / CONFERENCE_ABSTRACT / NO PUBLIC DATA LOCATED`

The CIAC 2025 abstract “Born to Regrow: Molecular and Cellular Pathways of Adult Plasticity and Regeneration” reports a distinct *O. vulgaris* arm-tip expression profile and an RT-qPCR set upregulated in arm tip and/or across healing, budding and re-growth.

The abstract does not name genes or provide sample sizes, controls, effect sizes, uncertainty, full methods or a public accession. It establishes that a stage-specific series apparently exists, not that a particular mechanism has been independently verified.

Primary source: [CIAC 2025 Book of Abstracts, page 157](https://www2.aeplan.co.jp/ciac2025/images/CIAC2025_Book_of_Abstracts_ZL7.pdf).

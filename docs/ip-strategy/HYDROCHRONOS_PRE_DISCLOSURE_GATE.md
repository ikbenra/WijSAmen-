## Goal

Preserve three options at once for as long as possible:

1. obtain a near-term paid evaluation, option, licence or assignment conversation;
2. prevent a later party from patenting the same disclosed mechanism and excluding Remco;
3. keep the resulting technology broadly usable rather than using exclusivity as the end goal.

These aims can coexist, but **not in the wrong order**.

## Hard finding

Europe normally has no general patent grace period. Public disclosure before the earliest filing date can destroy novelty. A defensive publication can block later patents on what it enables, but it also strongly limits Remco's own later patent options.

An i-DEPOT provides dated evidence that information existed in Remco's possession. It does not itself create an intellectual-property right and, while kept private, is not the public enabling disclosure needed to function as defensive prior art.

## Disclosure traffic light

### RED — keep private until the gate is decided

- working principle and geometry;
- self-correction or calibration mechanism;
- dimensions, tolerances, flow paths and material choices that make it work;
- CAD, build sequence, failure fixes and measured performance;
- the smallest claim nucleus that a skilled builder could reproduce;
- unpublished variants that close obvious workarounds.

Store this material only in a controlled private artifact with hashes and provenance. Do not put it in a public repository, public video, unrestricted pitch deck, marketplace listing or non-confidential partner message.

### AMBER — partner-safe, non-enabling summary

May describe:

- the problem and intended experience;
- observable benefits and target use;
- validation stage and requested partner capability;
- what a bounded test would measure;
- commercial forms to discuss: paid evaluation, time-limited option, licence or assignment.

Do not reveal enough to reproduce the mechanism. If a partner needs RED information, use a written confidentiality/evaluation framework reviewed before transmission.

### GREEN — already generic or intentionally public

- name, broad category and values;
- non-technical story;
- generic facts already in the public domain;
- evidence labels and explicit unknowns.

GREEN material must never imply that publication or patent filing happened.

## Four routes

### Route A — confidential partner-first

Use when rapid income matters most and the partner can evaluate commercial interest without receiving the full mechanism.

1. Send only AMBER information after Remco approves the exact recipient and message.
2. Ask whether they consider a paid feasibility review, evaluation option or defined licensing/assignment conversation.
3. Release RED information only after a suitable confidentiality and rights framework exists.
4. Record SOURCE_RECEIVED, SENT, REPLIED, AGREED and PAID separately.

**Benefit:** preserves patent and negotiation options.  
**Failure mode:** partner will not engage without enabling detail.  
**Stop rule:** no serial free disclosure to multiple recipients.

### Route B — file first, then deliberately open

A patent application is an exclusion right, but ownership does not require exclusion as the social strategy. After a competent filing, Remco could—subject to professional drafting—publish the mechanism and offer a broad royalty-free licence or defensive patent pledge while retaining leverage against an actor that tries to close the commons or sue users.

**Benefit:** can combine openness, attribution and defensive leverage.  
**Costs/unknowns:** drafting quality, prior art, fees, ownership, enforcement and the exact pledge language. A badly drafted application or pledge may provide little protection.

The EUIPO SME Fund 2026 advertises patent-related reimbursements, including up to EUR 1,000 for eligible national/European patent activities, but eligibility requires an EU-established SME and must be confirmed before incurring costs:

- [EUIPO SME Fund 2026](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/en/sme-corner/sme-fund/2026)
- [EUIPO patent-voucher FAQ](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/en/help-centre/websites/faq-sme-fund-2026)

Do not assume Remco personally qualifies.

### Route C — deliberate defensive publication

Choose only after consciously relinquishing relevant patent options.

A useful defensive publication needs:

- an unambiguous public date;
- a stable, searchable, independently retrievable copy;
- enough technical detail for a skilled person to reproduce the mechanism;
- drawings, variants and uses broad enough to cover obvious evasions;
- authorship and version hashes;
- no third-party confidential material.

**Benefit:** makes the enabled disclosure prior art against later filers.  
**Limits:** does not guarantee freedom to operate, cannot block patents on genuine improvements and offers no automatic enforcement budget or attribution mechanism.

### Route D — private dated evidence while deciding

An i-DEPOT or equivalent private dated record can strengthen proof of possession without public disclosure. Checked BOIP fees: EUR 37 for five years or EUR 53 for ten years; publication is separately priced. No purchase is authorized by this memo.

**Benefit:** inexpensive chronology/provenance evidence.  
**Limit:** not a patent, not public prior art while private, and not a substitute for a confidentiality agreement or freedom-to-operate analysis.

## Recommended sequence for Hydrochronos

1. **Recover source first.** Locate the exact original conversation/design, record message IDs/dates and separate original mechanism from later reconstruction.
2. **Freeze a private disclosure packet.** Mechanism, drawings, variants, evidence state and hashes; no public upload.
3. **Run a small prior-art screen.** Clock, clepsydra, fluid escapement, hydraulic oscillator, self-correcting timing and visual kinetic display families.
4. **Define the commercial nucleus.** What can a partner evaluate from AMBER information, and what must remain RED?
5. **Obtain one qualified confidential opinion.** Patentability/publication strategy and ownership, preferably before any enabling pitch.
6. **Choose one route explicitly:** confidential partner-first; file-first/open-licence; or deliberate defensive publication.
7. **Only then communicate or publish.**

## Fast-income experiment, prepared but not executed

A partner-facing offer can be framed as:

- a bounded evaluation of an unusual fluid-time mechanism and visual product concept;
- a fixed test question and short review period;
- no obligation to manufacture;
- possible paid evaluation, option, licence or assignment discussed separately;
- enabling details supplied only after confidentiality and rights are clear.

Success criterion: one qualified party accepts a defined paid or decision-bearing evaluation step.  
Failure criterion: repeated requests for full unpaid disclosure, no decision owner, or no defined commercial next step.  
Rollback: stop outreach, preserve the private packet and reconsider Route B or C.

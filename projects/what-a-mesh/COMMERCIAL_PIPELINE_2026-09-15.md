## Rights strategy

- Copyright in original expression exists without registration; registration/deposit can add evidence and may be required by particular recipients.
- Dharma explicitly requires proof of registration/copyright before a valid story submission.
- Low-cost formal evidence options to review before Dharma submission: BOIP i-DEPOT and/or WGA registration. No payment without explicit approval.
- Maintain underlying concept/originator position separately from paid screenwriting/development services where possible.
- Preferred first commercial transaction: bounded paid option or development agreement rather than permanent all-rights buyout.
- Avoid broad sequel/remake/series/interactive/merchandise transfers without explicit valuation and review.

## Conversion protocol when interest arrives

1. Read recipient's exact request/submission policy.
2. Send only requested material, version-numbered.
3. Ask whether NDA or submission release is required before deeper confidential material.
4. Clarify desired relationship: producer attachment, representation, option, co-development, screenplay commission, financing or distribution.
5. Do not imply exclusivity.
6. If financial/right terms appear, separate:
   - underlying concept/IP rights;
   - screenwriting/development fee;
   - creator credit;
   - creative involvement;
   - option term and renewal;
   - purchase price;
   - sequel/remake/series/derivative rights;
   - reversion if project is not produced.
7. Use specialist contract review before signing.

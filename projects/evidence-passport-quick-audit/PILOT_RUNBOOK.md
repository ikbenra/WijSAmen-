# Pilot Runbook — Evidence Passport Quick Audit

**Status:** INTERNAL / READY FOR HUMAN REVIEW / NOT SENT

This runbook makes one approved pilot executable without expanding scope or authority. It does not authorize outreach, pricing commitment, acceptance of work, or handling of sensitive data.

## 1. Approval gate

Before any external approach, record:

- exact recipient and why the fit is plausible;
- exact channel and final message;
- Remco's explicit approval;
- whether the fictional sample may be linked;
- workflow state: `PREPARED`, never `SENT` until transmission is independently verified.

Without all five: `ABSTAIN / NOT_SENT`.

## 2. Client-safe intake

Request only:

1. project goal in one sentence;
2. the decision the audit should clarify;
3. up to three sources the client owns or may share;
4. the most important claim or uncertainty;
5. any protectable detail that must be excluded;
6. desired delivery format.

Reject or pause material containing unnecessary medical, identity, account, financial, employment or third-party personal data. Do not request credentials, raw private-message archives or undisclosed IP.

## 3. Acceptance gate

Accept only if:

- one bounded project fits the published scope;
- rights to share the sources are stated;
- the task is analytical support, not certification or regulated advice;
- the sources are readable and the decision question is concrete;
- one correction round is sufficient.

If scope, rights or safety is unclear: `ABSTAIN / NOT_ACCEPTED`.

## 4. Delivery workflow

| Stage | Required evidence | State |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source receipt | file/link list plus provenance note | `SOURCE_RECEIVED` |
| Source verification | each supplied artifact opened or limitation recorded | `SOURCE_VERIFIED` or `SOURCE_LIMITED` |
| Draft | readable brief plus five-axis evidence passport | `PREPARED` |
| Quality check | alternative explanation, falsifier, cheapest test, stop rule | `CHECKED` |
| Delivery | exact artifact and transmission readback | `SENT` only after verification |
| Client response | literal reply or absence after agreed interval | `REPLIED` / `NO_REPLY_YET` |
| Interpretation | separate analysis of what the reply supports | `INTERPRETED` |

`SENT` never means true; `SOURCE_RECEIVED` never means verified.

## 5. Effort and economics log

Record minutes separately for intake, source review, drafting, checking, correction and administration. Also record:

- total effort;
- paid amount actually received;
- effective hourly revenue before costs;
- number and type of corrections;
- whether the client identified a real decision improvement;
- whether confidentiality or support demands exceeded the pilot scope.

## 6. Success and stop rules

Continue the pilot hypothesis only when at least one of the following occurs within five well-matched, individually approved approaches:

- one paid audit; or
- two credible sample/call requests tied to a real decision problem.

Pause or redesign if no credible interest appears, typical delivery exceeds two hours, buyers seek generic copywriting, or privacy/correction load makes €75 uneconomic.

## 7. First-run checklist

- [ ] recipient, channel and message explicitly approved;
- [ ] fictional sample reviewed for clarity;
- [ ] no confidential WijSAmen invention disclosed;
- [ ] outreach independently verified or left `NOT_SENT`;
- [ ] source rights and minimum-data intake confirmed;
- [ ] timer started only after complete source receipt;
- [ ] finished artifact quality-checked;
- [ ] delivery and payment states recorded separately;
- [ ] one-cycle learning captured without upgrading anecdotes into demand evidence.

## Outcome

A compact, source-linked audit of one innovation or project. It helps a founder, maker or small organisation see:

- what is actually supported by supplied sources;
- what remains a claim, inference, hypothesis or unknown;
- what has genuinely been executed;
- which dependency or assumption is most likely to block progress;
- the cheapest reversible next test and a clear stop criterion.

The customer receives one readable project page plus an evidence appendix. This is not a generic AI summary: provenance, verification, workflow state, authority and disclosure are kept on separate axes.

2. **Five-axis evidence passport**
   - provenance/responsibility;

3. **Decision section**

   - what evidence would falsify the current direction.

## Intake gate

Accept only when all are true:

- the client owns or may share the supplied material;
- the scope is one bounded project;
- no unnecessary personal, medical or third-party sensitive data is included;
- protectable details are either excluded or explicitly approved for this private review;
- the client understands this is analytical support, not legal, medical, investment, safety certification or engineering sign-off.

If these conditions fail: `ABSTAIN / NOT_ACCEPTED`.

## Quality gate

Before delivery:

- every material statement has a source/status label;
- `SENT`, `BUILT`, `TESTED` and `VERIFIED` are not conflated;
- at least one alternative explanation and one falsifier are included;
- no source is invented or silently upgraded;
- the result can be corrected without redoing the whole audit;
- the client can identify goal, difference, evidence, hypothesis, test and stop criterion.
